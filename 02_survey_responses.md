# Survey Responses – Fictional Company "Alpenwerk AG"

Fictional mid-sized Swiss manufacturer of industrial pumps (~120 employees). The sample covers 12 respondents from 4 teams. All names and data are invented. Answers are numbered by the questions in `01_survey_questions.md`.

## R01 – Sales, Key Account Manager
1. Sales, key account manager for DACH industrial customers.
2. Customer meetings, preparing quotes, chasing open orders with production.
3. "Project Atlas" – new pricing model; onboarding two new distributors.
4. Production planning (delivery dates), Engineering (technical questions in quotes).
5. I wait days for technical input on custom quotes. Only Marco in Engineering can answer most of them.

## R02 – Sales, Inside Sales
1. Sales, inside sales.
2. Order entry in ERP, answering customer emails, updating the CRM.
3. CRM clean-up.
4. Finance (invoices), Production planning.
5. We keep customer data in both the CRM and an Excel list, and they never match.

## R03 – Sales, Head of Sales
1. Head of Sales.
2. Forecasting, team management, key customer negotiations.
3. Project Atlas (lead); export expansion to Poland.
4. Management, Finance.
5. Hard to get reliable capacity data from Production for promises to customers.
